===========================================
STORMSHIELD RAIN JACKET
===========================================
Category: Outerwear
SKU: TP-JKT-001

The StormShield Rain Jacket is a fully waterproof, breathable hardshell built for serious wet-weather
hiking and backpacking. Constructed from a 3-layer ripstop nylon with a 20,000mm waterproof rating
and 15,000g/m2/24h breathability, it keeps rain and wind out while venting internal moisture during
high-output activity. Fully taped seams and a waterproof YKK zipper ensure no leaks at stress points.

Features an adjustable storm hood that fits over a climbing helmet, pit zips for quick venting,
and two chest pockets positioned above a hipbelt or backpack strap line for easy access while
wearing a pack. Packs down into its own chest pocket for storage. Available in sizes XS-XXL, and
in Slate Grey, Forest Green, and Sunrise Orange.

Best suited for: multi-day backpacking trips in wet climates, alpine hiking, trail running in
variable weather. Not recommended for extreme cold below 20F without an insulating layer underneath,
as this is a shell jacket without built-in insulation.

Care: Machine wash cold on gentle cycle with technical wash detergent (avoid regular detergent,
which degrades the waterproof coating). Reapply DWR (durable water repellent) treatment every
6-12 months with regular use.

ALPINEPEAK 2-PERSON TENT
===========================================
Category: Shelter
SKU: TP-TNT-002

A lightweight, freestanding backpacking tent designed for two people plus gear, weighing just 3.2 lbs
packed. Double-wall construction with a full-coverage rainfly rated to withstand sustained rain and
winds up to 35mph. Two doors and two vestibules mean each occupant has independent entry/exit and
gear storage without climbing over a tent-mate.

Aluminum DAC poles with a color-coded, intuitive setup that takes approximately 5 minutes solo.
Floor is a durable 20D nylon with a 3000mm waterproof rating, seam-taped throughout. Mesh body
panels maximize ventilation and stargazing on clear nights, with the rainfly providing full weather
protection when needed.

Best suited for: 3-season backpacking (spring through fall), thru-hiking, car camping where weight
still matters. Not rated for sustained heavy snow load - a 4-season tent is recommended for winter
mountaineering.

Included: tent body, rainfly, poles, stakes, guy lines, stuff sack, repair kit.

===========================================
SUMMITCARRY 65L BACKPACK
===========================================
Category: Packs
SKU: TP-PCK-003

A multi-day backpacking pack built around an adjustable torso-length suspension system, fitting
torso lengths from 16" to 20" with a single pack (adjustable via a ladder-lock system on the back
panel). 65 liters of capacity, expandable to 70L via a floating top lid, suitable for 3-5 day trips
depending on packing efficiency.

Ventilated back panel with a tensioned mesh suspended slightly off the pack body for airflow.
Load-bearing hip belt transfers the majority of weight to the hips rather than shoulders, rated
for loads up to 45 lbs comfortably, 55 lbs maximum. External attachment points for trekking poles,
ice axe, and a sleeping pad. Hydration reservoir compatible (reservoir sold separately).

Best suited for: multi-day backpacking, thru-hiking sections, extended trips requiring more gear
and food capacity. Overkill for day hikes or single-overnight trips - consider a 35-45L pack for
those instead.

Available in two frame sizes (Small/Medium and Medium/Large) - fit is determined by torso length,
not overall height, so measure before ordering.

===========================================
CLOUDNINE SLEEPING BAG
===========================================
Category: Sleep Systems
SKU: TP-SLP-004

A 20F (-6C) rated mummy-style sleeping bag insulated with 650-fill-power duck down, offering a
strong warmth-to-weight ratio for backpacking. Total packed weight of 2.1 lbs, compresses to the
size of a football in its included stuff sack.

Draft collar and draft tube along the zipper prevent heat loss at common escape points. Anatomical
foot box shape reduces pressure on toes compared to a straight taper. Two-way zipper allows venting
from the bottom if you run warm. Water-resistant (not waterproof) outer shell resists light
condensation inside a tent.

Best suited for: 3-season backpacking where the 20F rating provides a comfortable margin above
expected overnight lows. Down insulation loses effectiveness when wet - pair with a bivy or ensure
your tent's rainfly is in good condition. For consistently wet climates, consider a synthetic-fill
bag instead, which retains some insulating value even when damp.

Available in Regular (fits up to 6'0") and Long (fits up to 6'6") lengths.

===========================================
TRAILBLAZER HIKING BOOTS
===========================================
Category: Footwear
SKU: TP-BOOT-005

A mid-height waterproof hiking boot built for multi-day trips with a loaded pack. Waterproof-breathable
membrane keeps feet dry through stream crossings and wet trail conditions. Full-grain leather upper
with reinforced toe cap and heel counter for durability and support on uneven terrain.

Aggressive rubber outsole with 5mm lugs provides traction on loose rock, mud, and scree. Moderate
ankle support height suited for loaded-pack stability without the stiffness of a full mountaineering
boot. Cushioned EVA midsole with a semi-rigid nylon shank for torsional support under a heavy pack.

Best suited for: multi-day backpacking, uneven or rocky terrain, cooler-weather hiking where the
mid-height cut and waterproofing matter most. For warm-weather day hikes on maintained trails, a
lighter trail runner or low-cut hiking shoe will be more comfortable and require less break-in time.

Sizing runs true to standard US sizing; half sizes available. Recommend ordering your usual size,
or half a size up if you plan to wear thick hiking socks regularly.

TREKLIGHT TREKKING POLES
===========================================
Category: Accessories
SKU: TP-POLE-006

Carbon fiber trekking poles weighing 8.2 oz per pair, collapsing to 15 inches for pack storage via
a 3-section folding design (not telescoping), which deploys in seconds via an internal cord.
Adjustable from 100cm to 130cm to accommodate different heights and terrain (shorten for uphill,
lengthen for downhill).

Cork grip extensions below the main handle allow a lower hand position on steep ascents without
readjusting pole length. Interchangeable tip system includes rubber trail tips and carbide ice/snow
tips for different conditions. Flick-lock adjustment mechanism is more secure under load than
twist-lock designs and easier to operate with gloves on.

Best suited for: backpacking, trail running, and day hiking where reducing knee impact on descents
and improving stability on uneven terrain matters. Sold as a pair.

===========================================
HYDROFLOW WATER FILTER
===========================================
Category: Water Treatment
SKU: TP-FILT-007

A hollow-fiber membrane water filter rated to remove 99.9999% of bacteria and 99.9% of protozoa
(including Giardia and Cryptosporidium) down to 0.1 microns. Does not filter viruses - not
recommended for international travel to areas with viral water contamination risk without an
additional chemical or UV treatment step.

Filters up to 1,500 gallons before replacement is needed. Compatible with standard threaded water
bottles, included squeeze pouches, or can be used inline with a hydration reservoir. Backflush
syringe included for field cleaning to restore flow rate if clogging occurs.

Best suited for: backcountry water sources in North America and similar regions (streams, lakes)
where bacterial and protozoan contamination is the primary concern.

CAMPFIRE PORTABLE STOVE
===========================================
Category: Cooking
SKU: TP-STOVE-008

A compact canister camping stove weighing 3.1 oz, screwing directly onto standard threaded fuel
canisters (sold separately). Piezo igniter provides push-button ignition without matches or a
lighter, though a backup lighter is recommended in cold conditions where the igniter can be less
reliable. Boils approximately 1 liter of water in 3.5 minutes under normal conditions.

Folding pot supports stabilize cookware from 4 inches to 8 inches in diameter. Adjustable flame
control via a simple dial. Packs into a case smaller than a deck of cards.

Best suited for: solo or two-person backpacking meals, boiling water for dehydrated meals or
coffee. Not designed for simmering or slow-cooking - flame control is best suited to boiling
rather than a low, sustained simmer.

NIGHTWATCH HEADLAMP
===========================================
Category: Lighting
SKU: TP-LAMP-009

A rechargeable LED headlamp with 400 lumens maximum output, USB-C rechargeable via included cable
(3-hour full charge). Runtime ranges from 3 hours at maximum brightness to 40 hours at the lowest
setting. Red light mode preserves night vision and reduces disturbance to others in shared camping
areas.

Adjustable beam angle and a tilt-lock mechanism keep the light aimed where needed without slipping.
IPX6 water resistance handles rain and splashing but is not intended for submersion.

Best suited for: camping, backpacking, and trail running in low light. The USB-C rechargeable
design means no disposable batteries needed on trips where charging via a power bank or solar
charger is available; for extended trips without charging access, consider a battery-powered
model as a backup or primary option.

===========================================
Category: Camp Kitchen
SKU: TP-CLR-010

A 40-liter rotomolded insulated cooler designed for car camping and base camp use rather than

SOLARCHARGE POWER BANK
===========================================
Category: Electronics
SKU: TP-PWR-011

A 20,000mAh portable power bank with an integrated foldable solar panel for supplemental charging
during daylight hours. Solar charging alone is slow (full charge from empty via solar takes
approximately 2-3 full sunny days) and is best understood as a trickle-charge supplement to a wall
or USB charge before departure, not a primary charging method for a multi-day trip.

Two USB-A ports and one USB-C port allow charging multiple devices simultaneously. IP65 water and
dust resistance suitable for exposure during hiking, though not intended for submersion.

Best suited for: multi-day trips where keeping a phone, headlamp, or GPS device charged matters,
used primarily as a stored-charge power bank with solar as a secondary top-up method.
