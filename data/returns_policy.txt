TRAILPEAK OUTDOORS - RETURNS AND EXCHANGES POLICY

RETURN WINDOW

Most items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment
method. The 30-day window is calculated from the delivery date shown in tracking, not the order
date.

CONDITION REQUIREMENTS

Items must be unused, unworn, and in original condition with all original tags and packaging
attached. Footwear must be tried on indoors only on a clean surface - boots or shoes showing signs
of outdoor wear (dirt, scuffing, sole wear) cannot be accepted for return.

The following items cannot be returned under any circumstances due to hygiene and safety reasons:
- Water filters and water treatment products (HydroFlow Water Filter and similar items) once the
  packaging has been opened
- Undergarments and base layers worn against skin
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale" at time of purchase

GEAR THAT SHOWS SIGNS OF USE

Items showing clear signs of outdoor use - dirt, odor, wear on soles or fabric, or damage from use
- do not qualify for a standard return, even within the 30-day window. This policy exists because
returned gear showing use cannot be resold as new. If an item is defective, see the Warranty
Policy for a separate defect-based process that applies regardless of use.

HOW TO START A RETURN

Log into your account and select the order containing the item you'd like to return, then follow
the prompts to generate a prepaid return shipping label. Return shipping is free for defective or
incorrect items. For standard returns (change of mind, wrong size), a $7.99 return shipping fee is
deducted from the refund unless the original order qualified for free shipping, in which case
return shipping is also free.

REFUND TIMING

Once a returned item is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method and may take an additional 2-10 business days to
appear on a bank or credit card statement, depending on the customer's financial institution -
this portion of the timeline is outside TrailPeak Outdoors' control.

EXCHANGES

Size or color exchanges are handled as a return plus a new order, rather than a direct swap. This
means the new item is charged and shipped immediately, while the original item's refund follows
the standard return timeline above. We recommend this approach specifically so the replacement
size ships right away rather than waiting for the original return to be received first.

GIFT RETURNS

Items received as gifts can be returned for store credit without needing the original purchaser's
order information, provided the item is unused and within 30 days of the original delivery date.
Contact customer support with the gift recipient's information to process this type of return.
