TRAILPEAK OUTDOORS - SHIPPING POLICY

DOMESTIC SHIPPING (United States)

Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days, $6.99, or free on orders over $75.
Expedited Shipping: 2-3 business days, $14.99 flat rate.
Overnight Shipping: 1 business day, $29.99 flat rate, order must be placed before 1:00 PM EST.

Orders are processed within 1 business day of purchase, excluding weekends and holidays. Once an
order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email within 24 hours. Processing time is
separate from transit time - a Standard order placed on Friday afternoon will typically process
Monday and arrive 5-7 business days after that, not from the original order date.

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING

TrailPeak Outdoors currently ships to Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia.

Canada: 7-10 business days, $19.99 flat rate.
United Kingdom: 10-14 business days, $24.99 flat rate.
Australia: 12-16 business days, $27.99 flat rate.

International orders may be subject to customs duties, taxes, or import fees determined by the
destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the customer and are not included
in the shipping cost or collected at checkout. TrailPeak Outdoors has no control over customs
processing times, which can occasionally extend delivery beyond the estimated window.

We do not currently ship to any destination outside the four countries listed above.

ORDER TRACKING

Tracking information is sent via email once a label is created. Please allow up to 24 hours after
receiving a tracking number for the carrier's system to display movement - a tracking number that
appears inactive immediately after issuance is normal and does not indicate a problem.

SHIPPING DELAYS AND LOST PACKAGES

If a package shows as delivered but has not been received, please wait 24 hours, as carriers
occasionally mark packages delivered slightly before physical delivery. If the package still has
not arrived after 24 hours, contact customer support with your order number so we can file a
claim with the carrier.

If a package appears stuck in transit for more than 5 business days beyond the original estimated
delivery window, contact customer support for assistance - do not assume the package is lost
before this window has passed, as most delayed packages do eventually arrive.

ADDRESS CHANGES

Address changes can only be made before an order ships. Once a tracking number has been issued,
TrailPeak Outdoors cannot modify the delivery address - any address correction at that point must
be handled directly with the carrier, and success is not guaranteed.

SPLIT SHIPMENTS

Orders containing items from different warehouse locations may arrive in separate packages on
different days. This does not incur additional shipping charges - the flat rate or free-shipping
threshold applies to the order as a whole, not per package.
