TRAILPEAK OUTDOORS - WARRANTY POLICY

GENERAL WARRANTY COVERAGE

TrailPeak Outdoors warrants its products against manufacturing defects in materials and
workmanship for the period specified per product category below, starting from the original
purchase date. This warranty covers manufacturing defects only - it does not cover normal wear
and tear, damage from misuse, or damage from accidents.

WARRANTY PERIODS BY CATEGORY

Outerwear (jackets, insulated gloves): 2 years
Shelter (tents): 3 years
Packs (backpacks): Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
Sleep Systems (sleeping bags): 2 years
Footwear (hiking boots): 1 year
Accessories (trekking poles): 2 years
Water Treatment (filters): 1 year, covers the filter housing only - filter cartridge performance
  degradation over its rated 1,500-gallon lifespan is expected and not a defect
Cooking (stoves): 2 years
Lighting (headlamps): 2 years
Camp Kitchen (coolers): 5 years
Electronics (power banks): 1 year

WHAT IS COVERED

Seam failure not caused by damage, zipper failure under normal use, defective waterproof coating
that fails to repel water when new, structural failure of poles, frames, or hardware under normal
use conditions, and premature failure of electronic components under normal use.

WHAT IS NOT COVERED

Damage from improper care (see individual product care instructions in the product catalog),
damage from accidents, falls, or abrasion during use, normal fading or wear from extended use,
damage from improper storage (such as storing a tent wet, which can cause mildew not covered
under warranty), and damage from use outside a product's intended purpose (for example, using a
3-season tent in sustained heavy snow load).

Products purchased from unauthorized third-party resellers are not eligible for warranty service
through TrailPeak Outdoors directly.

HOW TO FILE A WARRANTY CLAIM

Contact customer support with your order number, a description of the issue, and photos showing
the defect. Claims are typically reviewed within 5 business days. If a claim is approved,
TrailPeak Outdoors will repair the item, replace it with the same or a comparable current model
if the original is discontinued, or issue store credit, at our discretion depending on the nature
of the defect and product availability.

A proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt) is required for all warranty claims. Products
registered to an account at time of purchase do not need a separate receipt, as the order history
serves as proof of purchase.

RELATIONSHIP TO THE RETURNS POLICY

The Returns Policy's 30-day window and "no signs of use" requirement apply to standard returns
for reasons unrelated to product defects (wrong size, changed mind). If an item develops a defect
after the 30-day return window, or shows signs of normal use, it may still be eligible under this
Warranty Policy instead - a defective item is not disqualified from support just because it has
been used, since defects often only become apparent through use.
